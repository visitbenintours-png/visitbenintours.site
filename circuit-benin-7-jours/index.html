Detailed day-by-day itinerary

Day 1

Ganvié et Ouidah

Private hotel pickup with driver-guide; transfer to Abomey-Calavi; private motorized boat tour of Ganvié’s lakeside neighbourhoods, fishing techniques, akadjas, floating market and Lovers’ Canal; continue to Ouidah for lunch, Kpassè Sacred Forest, Python Temple, Auction Square, Slave Route, Tree of Forgetting, Tree of Return and Door of No Return; return to Cotonou.

Day 2

Porto-Novo et Adjarra

Discover Porto-Novo, the political capital, its museums and architectural heritage including the Afro-Brazilian Great Mosque; continue to Adjarra for traditional crafts and an excursion to the Black River; return to Cotonou or overnight according to the chosen arrangement.

Day 3

Grand-Popo

Discover Seigbé village, take a boat trip on the Mono River, visit Salt Island and the sea turtle conservation centre, and explore local landscapes and traditions.

Day 4

Abomey

Visit the Royal Palaces of Abomey, explore the history of the Kingdom of Danxomè, visit the Historical Museum and discover the history of the former kings and Fon culture.

Day 5

Dassa-Zoumè

Discover the Dassa hills, visit the Notre-Dame d’Arigbo grotto and explore the region’s natural and cultural heritage.

Day 6

Destination to be selected

This day is intentionally open for a destination to be added according to your interests. Visits and accommodation will be agreed before the circuit is confirmed.

Day 7

Final discovery and return to Cotonou

Choose a final destination together according to your interests and available time, then return to Cotonou and transfer to your hotel or agreed drop-off point.
