A week is enough to see Bénin's essential destinations without feeling like you're constantly packing the car. This itinerary keeps driving distances reasonable and leaves room to actually experience each place, not just tick it off.

## Day 1 — Arrive in Cotonou

Settle in, adjust to the heat and pace, and get an easy introduction with a half-day around Dantokpa Market and the beach. <a href="/cotonou.html">See our Cotonou guide →</a>

## Day 2 — Ganvié

An early departure for the stilt village of Ganvié — sunrise pirogue tour, floating market, and back to Cotonou by late morning with the rest of the day free. <a href="/ganvie.html">See our Ganvié guide →</a>

## Day 3 — Ouidah

A full day tracing the Slave Route, the Door of No Return, the Python Temple and the Sacred Forest of Kpassè — one of the most meaningful days of the trip for most travellers. <a href="/ouidah.html">See our Ouidah guide →</a>

## Day 4 — Grand-Popo

Move to the coast for a slower day — wild Atlantic beach, a Mono River pirogue crossing, and a genuinely restful night away from any city noise. <a href="/grand-popo.html">See our Grand-Popo guide →</a>

## Day 5 — Abomey

Drive inland to Abomey for the Royal Palaces, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the history of the Kingdom of Dahomey — one of West Africa's most significant historical stops. <a href="/abomey.html">See our Abomey guide →</a>

## Day 6 — Dassa-Zoumè or Porto-Novo

Depending on your interests: Dassa-Zoumè's sacred granite hills for a quieter, scenic day, or Porto-Novo's Afro-Brazilian architecture and museums for more culture and history. <a href="/dassa-zoume.html">See our Dassa-Zoumè guide →</a> · <a href="/porto-novo.html">See our Porto-Novo guide →</a>

## Day 7 — Return to Cotonou, departure

A relaxed final morning, last-minute souvenir shopping, and transfer to the airport.

## Why this order works

This route minimises backtracking — Ganvié and Ouidah are close to Cotonou, Grand-Popo and Abomey form a natural inland loop, and the final day brings you back to your departure point without a long final drive. Every leg is under half a day's travel.
